# Advertising and billboards

Commencement's first advertising system (archival Coca-Cola, Kodak and Mitsukoshi artwork on the Times Square, SoHo and Shibuya corridors, with an inspector and a real-brand switch) was removed in Step 10 together with those locations. It remains in git history and on the main branch, including its source manifest.

Washington Square has no billboards and none are planned. The satirical billboards once planned for Step 8 were dropped on 9 Oct 2026: the game's commentary on NYU will come indirectly, through real, documented events that a story is built around later. The research for that is `RESEARCH/nyu-history.md`, a sourced timeline with no in-game content of its own.

Real storefront signs and NYU's own flags and banners stay, as they are part of the real streets.

Flags, banners, signs and plaques on the campus are separate signage decals listed in `dist/assets/signage/manifest.js`; see CAMPUS.md.
