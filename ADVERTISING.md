# Advertising and billboards

NightView's first advertising system (archival Coca-Cola, Kodak and Mitsukoshi artwork on the Times Square, SoHo and Shibuya corridors, with an inspector and a real-brand switch) was removed in Step 10 together with those locations. It remains in git history and on the main branch, including its source manifest.

Washington Square has no advertising yet. Step 8 of the plan builds satirical billboards around the square, aimed at NYU policies and decisions and drawn from Washington Square News reporting, with their own manifest recording every source. The rules for them: no named individuals, no deaths, mental-health tragedies or assault cases, nothing on or near the Brown Building or the Triangle Fire memorial, no real businesses satirised, and no NYU torch emblem or other logo artwork (plain lettering in NYU violet only).

Flags, banners, signs and plaques on the campus are separate signage decals listed in `dist/assets/signage/manifest.js`; see CAMPUS.md.
