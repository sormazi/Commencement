# The edge of Union Square (Step 4B, Tier A, 10 Oct 2026)

The study area stops at 14th St, but the NYC data reaches into Union Square, so its statues are placed at the positions OpenStreetMap gives. Built in `dist/campus/landmarks/union-square.js`.

Barre granite pedestal 12'2" x 7'9" in plan, 15' high, rounded at both ends, with a base course, a mid band, a cornice and a raised tablet on each long side. | Wikipedia (dimensions, makers); Commons photographs (CC0, Jul 2026 and Nov 2024) |

Photographs were looked at only and are not saved in the repository.

Estimates to check on site or on Street View: Lafayette's facing (taken as west, into the park) and Lincoln's (taken as south, along the park's axis with the flagstaff and Washington); the heights of the Lafayette and Lincoln pedestals, read off the photographs; the size of the Gandhi plinth.

Not yet modelled at the edge of the square: the south plaza's steps and paving pattern, the park's north pavilion (it renders as a plain brick block), the Union Square Greenmarket stalls.
