# Field guide notes

Entries for the in-game field guide (Step 9), collected as the world is built. Facts only, with sources.

## The Washington Square fountain

- Runs in the warm months: NYC Parks turns it on in late April (17 to 27 April in the years reported) and off in mid-October, sometimes later (still running 18 October 2016; into mid-November 2011). The game runs it from 25 April to 15 October.
- Daily hours are not published. The game runs it from 8 am to 9 pm and keeps it off at night.
- A tall central jet rises from a stepped pedestal; smaller jets arc in from spouts on the rim.
- In 2126 it is dry: a stained basin, moss in the bottom step, a young tree rooted in the floor.
