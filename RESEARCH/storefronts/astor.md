# Astor Place and 770 Broadway storefronts (second pass, 10 Oct 2026)

Google Street View, Apr 2026 captures, looked at only and not saved. Records in `dist/campus/storefronts/astor.js`; interiors in `dist/campus/storefronts/insides.js`. Plain lettering only; every logo is an empty slot in the signage manifest.

## Astor Place, north side (Broadway to Lafayette St)
- 752 Broadway / 4 Astor Pl, brick corner building: residential entrance with ASTOR PLACE on the frieze, cast-iron ground floor. No shop on the Astor Place front.
- Same lot, dark terracotta building: **Pret A Manger**. Charcoal flat awning lettered "Freshly prepared ★ Astor Place" in white, bronze-coloured frames, burgundy blade sign (not modelled), PRET etched on the glass.
- 21 Astor Pl, Clinton Hall (cream brick, red brick arches): its own arched entrance at the west end; **DashMart** behind white-papered windows, under red striped bands lettered DASH MART in white; at the Lafayette end a one-storey glass pavilion with a sloping canopy, empty and boarded with plywood (modelled as a boarded shopfront; the pavilion's own roof is not modelled yet).

## Astor Place, south side (740 Broadway, the red brick and cast-iron Astor Building)
From Lafayette St towards Broadway:
- **Raising Cane's** at the Lafayette corner: red sign panels with white lettering, red corner awning, stone piers.
- An empty shop with bare glass.
- The office lobby (stone-framed entrance; no record).
- **Juice Generation**: black fascia, white lettering.
- **TMPL Fitness** at the Broadway corner: gold letters on the black fascia (David Barton Gym until about 2016).

## 770 Broadway (Wanamaker Annex)
- **Lafayette St front: Wegmans** along the whole front. Dark green framed glazing under the stone arches, gold italic lettering on the stone above the glass, and a projecting green entrance vestibule with gold trim ("Wegmans since 1916" on its side, not modelled). Interior: a 26 m deep grocery with long aisles under strip lights.
- **Broadway front** (north to south): the 770 Broadway office lobby under a purple name panel; NYU's science building windows covered in purple graphics (modelled as purple panels, no text); **Bank of America** in dark teal frames with a white lettered band inside the glass.
- The Tier A spec's tall glazed base became plain stone behind these records (`landmarks/tiera-kit.js`, slug wanamaker). The E 9th St front was not surveyed yet.
