# E 8th St storefronts, University Pl to Mercer St (second pass, 10 Oct 2026)

Google Street View, Apr 2026 captures, looked at only and not saved. Records in `dist/campus/storefronts/e8th.js`. Positions along each front are estimated from the viewpoints to about two metres. Plain lettering only; every logo is an empty slot.

## South side, 50 E 8th St (Greene St to Mercer St)
From the Greene St corner going east: **Heavenly Market & Cafe** (dark green fascia, green lettering) at the corner; **Choji** (black fascia, white lettering); the apartment lobby; a **nail salon** under a blue awning; **Mr. Bubbly** (white sign, black awning, closed with a retail-for-lease notice in the window); **Rokstar Chicken** (red perforated panel, white lettering); a shop under an **orange awning** whose name is not legible on the imagery; a planted court in front of the apartments; **LaoMa Spicy** (black fascia, white script, red menu panels) at the Mercer corner.

## North side (the apartment blocks addressed on E 9th St)
- 30 E 9th St front, near Greene St: **Subway**, then a **coffee shop** whose sign is partly hidden by a tree, then a residential entrance with a small pediment.
- 40 E 9th St front, Greene to Mercer: an **urgent care clinic** (red sign) at the Greene end, two empty shops (one with a blue University Village "retail space for lease" banner, one with tinted glass), a **tea shop** with a green sign, and **Just Salad** (brick-brown band with the name in small lettering, big glass).
- 60 E 9th St front, near Mercer: an empty shop with blue for-lease panels, then **Brooklyn Bagel & Coffee Company** (green lettering on a pale band).

## Known gaps
- The urgent care, tea shop and coffee shop names need a closer look.
- The upper floors of 50 E 8th St come out pale from the facade rules; the real building is red-brown brick.
- 60 E 8th St (Georgetown Plaza, Mercer St to Broadway) not surveyed yet.
