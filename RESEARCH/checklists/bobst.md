## Facade

- [x] Red sandstone, laid in small patchy slabs (C bobst_2022, 2022b, 2026), as a texture with shallow joints.
- [x] Ground floor: square sandstone piers in the face plane, glazing set back 2.4 m, soffit over the recess, centre mullions and transoms.
- [x] Continuous spandrel band over the ground floor.
- [x] Upper piers with a shallow rounded front running to the roof, each ending at the band in a pale quarter-dome foot (the half-moon shapes in C bobst_2026 and bobst_entrance_2011).
- [x] Glazed bays (north and west faces): recessed 0.9 m, two window columns per bay split by a stone mullion, thin stone spandrel and a transom at each floor (C bobst_2022b).
- [~] Solid bays (south and east faces): stone infill with one deep slot window up the middle (C bobst_2019, bobst_south_2011). Exact slot width and which bays are solid not verified.
- [x] Attic band: deep square opening in every bay under the roofline (C bobst_2019, bobst_south_2011).
