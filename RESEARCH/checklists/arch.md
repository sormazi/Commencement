## Proportions (from documented totals, zone heights measured off C photos)

- [x] Total height 77 ft / 23.47 m (LPC; NYC footprint height 20.42 m is to the roof deck behind the attic parapet).
- [x] Opening 30 ft / 9.14 m wide, 47 ft / 14.33 m to the crown of the intrados (LPC). Semicircular arch, springing at 9.76 m.
- [x] Plan 19.1 × 7.0 m from the NYC footprint; pier shafts modelled 18.6 × 6.4 m, cornice overhang brings the top to the footprint outline.
- [x] Piers 4.73 m wide each side of the opening (pier : opening ≈ 1 : 1.93, matches C arch_bright_2026 and arch_north).
- [~] Zone heights: plinth 0–0.6, statue pedestals to 2.9, impost 9.06–9.76, architrave 15.6, frieze 16.45, cornice 17.75–18.95, attic to 23.47. Measured from C photos with perspective; ±0.3 m.

## Carved figures and reliefs

- [x] Statue group, east pier, north face: *Washington as Commander-in-Chief, accompanied by Fame and Valor* (MacNeil, 1916). Cloaked figure in tricorn, both hands on a sword held point-down; two standing allegorical figures in relief behind; arched niche behind his head (C arch_washington_war).
- [x] Statue group, west pier, north face: *Washington as President, accompanied by Wisdom and Justice* (Calder, 1918). Bareheaded, civilian coat, left hand on a book resting on a pedestal at his left; Wisdom (helmeted) and Justice (sword, scales) in relief (C arch_washington_peace, arch_2022).
- [~] Hand holding an open book (inscribed EXITUS ACTA PROBAT) above the Peace group: modelled as a block book and hand, no lettering yet.
- [~] Statue pedestals: projecting 1.05 m, base and cap mouldings, plinth under each figure. Pedestals are now solid for the car.
- [~] Spandrel Victories (MacMonnies, 1895), two per face: reclining draped bodies along the extrados, head toward the keystone, arm reaching forward with a wreath, wings as fans of separate feathers filling the corner toward the pier. Faces and drapery are simplified; they read as winged figures from the plaza, not up close.
- [x] Eagles over both keystones (Martiny, 1892): displayed, wings raised, separate primary feathers, head turned, tail fan, talons on the keystone.
- [x] Keystone consoles on both faces: scrolled console 2.1 m high with volutes and an acanthus leaf.
- [~] Trophy panels on the upper piers (both faces): sunk panel with a roundel and shield, plumed helmet over crossed spears, crossed swords over fasces, laurel sprays. Arrangement matches C photos; individual trophies are generic.
- [x] Frieze: eight laurel wreaths with stars per face, linked by laurel and oak sprays.
- [x] Attic side panels: wreath with streaming ribbons and palm fronds.

## Mouldings and architecture (all real geometry)

- [x] Base: plinth and torus moulding around each pier.
- [x] Impost: Greek-key (meander) fascia under an ovolo and corona, wrapping all four sides of each pier including inside the opening (C arch_2022, arch_closeup).
- [x] Archivolt on both faces: stepped fasciae with bead mouldings, 0.84 m wide.
- [x] Coffered vault soffit: 9 coffers across the depth × 23 around the arc, each with a stepped two-stage recess and a rosette; plain soffit bands at both faces (C arch_closeup, arch_south).
- [x] Architrave in three fasciae with a crowning ovolo, running round all four sides.
- [x] Cornice: bed ovolo, dentil band (0.13 m blocks at 0.22 m), scrolled modillions at 0.62 m under a 0.78 m corona, cyma recta crown; all four sides.
- [x] Attic: base moulding, crowning cornice, raised moulded frame around the inscription tablet.
- [x] Coursed ashlar joints (0.61 m courses) as a normal map on the marble.
- [x] Crown fixing block under the soffit.
- [x] Plain sunk panels on the east and west side faces (C arch_2022 shows these sides plain; earlier draft had ornament there, removed).

## Inscriptions

- [x] North attic: LET US RAISE A STANDARD TO WHICH THE WISE AND THE HONEST CAN REPAIR. THE EVENT IS IN THE HAND OF GOD. WASHINGTON. Line breaks are approximate (C arch_south too oblique to read the breaks).
- [x] South attic: TO COMMEMORATE THE ONE HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY / OF THE INAUGURATION OF GEORGE WASHINGTON / AS FIRST PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES (line breaks from C arch_closeup).
