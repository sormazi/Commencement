# Silver Towers (University Village): detail checklist

Model: `dist/campus/landmarks/silver-towers.js`. Silver Towers I (BIN 1087825, 100 Bleecker St) and II (BIN 1083218, 110 Bleecker St), NYU; 505 LaGuardia Place (BIN 1008241, co-op, built with them). I. M. Pei & Associates, 1964–67. NYC landmark 2008.
Plans and heights from the NYC 3D Building Model (2014). Sources: L = LPC designation report 2300; C = Commons photos (`RESEARCH/reference/commons/`, st_*); D = NYC data; K = estimate.

Status: **[x] modelled** · **[~] approximation** · **[ ] open**.

## Massing

- [x] Three identical 30-storey towers in a pinwheel round a 100 × 100 ft lawn (L p. 8; lawn ring from OSM).
- [x] Plan of each tower, main roof at 82.5–82.8 m and rooftop bulkheads to 89–92 m (D, 3D model). Colinear faces that the model splits are merged.
- [x] 29 upper floors over a ground storey of about twice the floor height: 5.6 m ground storey and 2.65 m floors (K, fitted to the 82.5 m roof and checked on C st_j04).

## Facades (L pp. 8, 15; C st_uv002, st_j04, st_flickr2)

- [x] Warm buff, smooth cast-in-place concrete (L: "comparable to sandstone or limestone"; not board-formed).
- [x] Waffle grid on every face of the shaft: eight bays on the long faces, four on the ends (L "four or eight"; counted on C st_uv002 and st_j04). Bay pitch 2.30 m (K: eight bays over the 18.6 m grid).
- [x] T-shaped columns, narrower at the front than at the glass (a wedge in plan), 0.75 m deep; floor slabs at every floor; parapet band.
- [x] Each bay: a pair of sliding windows over a ventilation grille; the reveal shade is baked into the bay texture, with curtains and lit rooms varied across a 4 × 4 tile.
- [x] Beside each grid: a slot of narrow windows (0.55 m), then a smooth windowless shear wall in vertical panels with floor joints. The set-back faces of the plan are shear wall too.
- [~] Which end of each face takes the shear wall: the end next to the plan's jog (K, from the 3D-model outline; agrees with C st_uv002 and st_j04 for the faces they show).

## Ground storey (L p. 15)

- [x] Entrance fronts with a deep arcade: the columns run to the pavement; lobby glass 3.2 m back; tan brick walls at both ends; entrance pavilion with dark aluminium frame. Tower I faces the lawn (west), tower II and 505 face north (L p. 15: 505's lobby faces north).
- [x] Raised address numerals "100", "110", "505" on the brick wall right of each entrance, and the circa-1970s "SILVER TOWERS, NEW YORK UNIVERSITY" plaque on towers I and II. The NYU torch is not drawn.
- [x] Secondary faces: large windows over a raised concrete base between the columns.
- [~] Arcade depth and pavilion size estimated (K).

## Site

- [x] **Bust of Sylvette** at the south-east corner of the lawn, opposite the entrance to 110 Bleecker (L p. 14), with the small brass plaque on a low concrete pedestal. **Represented by a neutral placeholder only:** a plain slab of the sculpture's overall size (36 ft high, 20 ft wide, 12.5 in thick) on a low plinth. The sculpture is a protected artwork, so its form is not modelled. It never appears in billboards or banners either. Solid for collision.
- [x] The OpenStreetMap node for the sculpture lies 115 m further south, near Houston St; it is not used, and its collision circle is replaced by the plinth.
- [ ] Granite-block drive along former Wooster St, concrete bollards, cantilevered bench on Bleecker, flagpoles, spiral benches, playground, five-globe lamp posts (L p. 14). For the street-furniture pass.
