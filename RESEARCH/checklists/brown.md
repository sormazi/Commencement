# Brown Building and the Triangle Shirtwaist Factory Fire Memorial: detail checklist

Model: `dist/campus/landmarks/brown.js` (BIN 1008823, 23–29 Washington Place at Greene St; former Asch Building, John Woolley, 1900–01; LPC LP-2128). **Preserved**: the group carries `userData.preserve = true` and the registry entry `preserve: true`, so Phase 3 decay, jokes, billboards and banners skip the building and the memorial (Avi, 2026-10-07).

Status: **[x] modelled** · **[~] approximation** · **[ ] open**. Sources: LPC = LP-2128 description (quoted); C = Commons (brown_west and brown_entrance by Beyond My Ken 2011; brown_memorial_2024 by Eejjmm8888, 30 Sep 2024); W = Untapped Cities, 11 Oct 2023, on the memorial; SV = Google Street View (Apr 2026, Washington Pl at 40.72986 −73.99545), looked at, not saved.

## Proportions

- [x] Lot "101 feet along Washington Place and 100 feet along Greene Street" (LPC); fronts 31.0 m and 31.3 m on the NYC footprint.
- [x] Ten storeys plus penthouse; main cornice 42.9 m, penthouse 45.9 m (3D model).
- [x] "Tripartite arrangement of stories with a two-story base, a seven-story mid-section, and a one-story top."
- [x] Washington Pl "twelve bays with windows grouped in a 3-1-2-2-1-3 pattern between heavy piers"; Greene St "3-1-1-1-1-3".

## Base

- [x] "Five massive piers that are treated as banded pilasters" on Washington Pl (seven on Greene St): polished granite bases, limestone shafts with recessed granite bands, terra-cotta capitals.
- [x] Storefronts with "polished granite bulkheads and louvered metal panels" and glass-block infill (drawn as the round glass lenses seen in C brown_entrance and SV), signboard band.
- [x] Washington Pl entrance in the west bay: cast-iron piers, dark doors, "BROWN BUILDING, BIOLOGY, CHEMISTRY, 29" panel, "NEW YORK UNIVERSITY" on the frieze above.
- [x] Greene St entrance in the northernmost bay, simpler (no frieze lettering).
- [x] Second storey: each major bay "divided into three window bays by cast-iron mullions"; frieze with a fret (Greek key) band and cornice over the base.

## Mid-section and top

- [x] Third storey: corner pavilions "enriched with banded rustication and by terra-cotta cartouches"; cornice with leaf-and-dart (drawn as a moulded band).
- [x] Tan brick fourth to ninth storeys "accented by stone sill and lintel courses"; "recessed brick panels bordered by terra-cotta egg-and-dart moldings" in the pavilion spandrels and between the 5th/6th and 7th/8th storeys in the centre.
- [x] "Fluted Corinthian cast iron columns" between the windows of the centre bays (as plain round columns).
- [x] Tenth storey: arched windows with keystones; pilasters in the outer bays.
- [x] "Strongly projecting bracketed galvanized-iron crowning cornice" with brackets and modillions.
- [x] Penthouse and roof from the 3D model.

## Triangle Shirtwaist Factory Fire Memorial (2023)

- [x] Textured stainless-steel ribbon, about 2 ft high, running along both fronts over the storefronts and turning the corner (W; C brown_memorial_2024).
- [~] The names and ages of the 146 workers are cut through the ribbon (W). Drawn as rows of perforation, not legible text, so that no name is reproduced wrongly.
- [x] Dark reflective panel at hip height below, where the cut names are reflected (W).
- [ ] The second phase that carries the ribbon up the corner to the ninth-floor sill (W) is not modelled: it was not visible in the Apr 2026 Street View or the 2024 Commons photo.
