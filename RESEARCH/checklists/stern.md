# Tisch Hall and the Kaufman Management Center (NYU Stern): detail checklist

Model: `dist/campus/landmarks/stern.js`. Henry Kaufman Management Center, 44 W 4th St (BIN 1078952; completed about 1992, 1990 in PLUTO). Tisch Hall, 40 W 4th St (BIN 1077346; Philip Johnson and Richard Foster, 1972). Both face Gould Plaza.
Volumes from the NYC 3D Building Model (2014). Sources: D = NYC 3D model and footprint data; C = Commons photos (`RESEARCH/reference/commons/`, stern_*; 2012–19); SV = Google Street View, looked at, not saved; K = estimate.

Status: **[x] modelled** · **[~] approximation** · **[ ] open**.

## Kaufman Management Center

- [x] Stepped volume to 51.7 m with the half-round rotunda on the plaza (D).
- [x] Pale stone panels; a dense grid of windows, each split by a mullion, 2.45 m bays, 3.95 m floors (C stern_full, stern_1; K for the dimensions).
- [x] The two lowest storeys in big openings with red gridded frames between stone piers (C stern_kmc, stern_plaza).
- [x] Rotunda (C stern_kmc, stern_full): nine columns two storeys high in front of a recessed glazed lobby; a stone band and a ring of windows; the band lettered LEONARD N. STERN SCHOOL OF BUSINESS with a cornice; a sloping glass dome with radial ribs and a stone rim.
- [x] HENRY KAUFMAN MANAGEMENT CENTER sign over the doors; two plain violet flags on angled poles. The NYU torch on the flags is not drawn.
- [~] Rotunda radius from the 3D model (9.1 m); column count, band heights and the dome slope are estimates.
- [ ] Red spandrel accents under the top floors (C stern_kmc).

## Tisch Hall

- [x] Volume to 44.6 m: the main roof at 36.8 m inside a sandstone frame that rises to 44.6 m (D). The top storey is drawn as dark glass deep inside that frame (SV Apr 2026, first pass: "dark recessed top floor").
- [x] Red sandstone in the same treatment as Bobst; a regular grid of deep windows, 2.5 m bays, 3.6 m floors (C stern_full, stern_grad; K for the dimensions).
- [x] Ground storey: the glazed entrance on the plaza in a pale stone frame lettered NYU STERN SCHOOL OF BUSINESS, and pale green glass panels in alternate bays (C stern_nyc, stern_full).
- [~] Window pairing and the vertical piers every few bays (C stern_full) are simplified to a regular grid.

## Gould Plaza and the shed

- [x] The plaza's W 4th St front is behind the construction shed and fence placed from Street View (Apr 2026); see `sheds.js` and the shed row in the inventory. The plaza paving with its glass-pane walkway (C stern_full, 2019) is not modelled while the site is under construction.
