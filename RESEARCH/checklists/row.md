# The Row, Washington Square North: detail checklist

Model: `dist/campus/landmarks/row.js`. East of Fifth Av: Nos. 1–13 (1832–33); west of Fifth Av: Nos. 19–26. Source text: LPC Greenwich Village Historic District report (LP-0489) pp.52–57, quoted below.

Status: **[x] modelled** · **[~] approximation** · **[ ] open**. C = Commons (row and row_19_26 / row_19_26_east by Beyond My Ken 2011; kimmel_esb by Markus Poessel 2008); D = NYC footprints and 3D model.

## Layout

- [x] Each house front built on its footprint's park-facing edge (all at the same street offset, D). Nos. 7–13 share one 67 m front (seven houses), Nos. 1–6 one footprint each; Nos. 19–26 per footprint.
- [x] Fronts set back from the sidewalk behind front gardens: the leases put the fronts "12 feet back of and parallel with" the street; the railing runs along the sidewalk 3.6 m in front.
- [x] Volume behind the fronts from the 3D model.

## Nos. 1–13 (east of Fifth)

- [x] Red brick ("conservatively built of brick in the time-tested Flemish bond"), three bays per house.
- [x] Raised rusticated basement with framed basement windows.
- [x] Entrance porticoes: "the two exterior columns, supporting the entablature, are fluted Doric", white marble; panelled door with a fanlight.
- [x] White marble front steps with iron stoop railings.
- [x] Floor-length parlour windows; six-over-six sash with white frames and muntins.
- [x] "Most of the window lintels are pedimented with level shoulders over the bearing points and include the diminutive stone cornice"; marble sills.
- [x] Roof entablature with "low attic windows neatly cut into the frieze above the taenia molding", guttae at the taenia, attic windows screened (bars standing in for the Greek fret screens), cornice.
- [x] Balustrades at the roofline on the east houses.
- [x] Nos. 7–13: "a cornice which was made higher at a later date": an extra storey under the entablature.
- [~] No. 3: "remodeled in 1884" (Queen Anne): drawn without a portico, with a large studio window and a gable. Details not checked against a photo.
- [x] "The continuous Greek Revival railing running along the sidewalk", with posts and finials at each house (double gates simplified to posts).

## Nos. 19–26 (west of Fifth)

- [x] Same house kit with variants seen in C row_19_26: brownstone lintels and stoops, dark green shutters, iron balconies on the second floor.
- [~] Individual remodellings (No. 19's store front, No. 21's 1928 storey and penthouse, LPC) are not modelled.
