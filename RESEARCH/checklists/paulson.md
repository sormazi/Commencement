# John A. Paulson Center: detail checklist

Model: `dist/campus/landmarks/paulson.js` (BIN 1090263, 181 Mercer St; completed 2022). Replaces the estimated three-block massing in `campus-world.js`.
The building postdates the 2014 NYC 3D model. Sources: D = NYC footprint; W = CTBUH skyscrapercenter.com (complex 5988) and world-architects.com; C = Commons photos (`RESEARCH/reference/commons/`, pc_*; 2023–26); SV = Google Street View (Mercer and Bleecker, Apr 2026), looked at, not saved; K = estimate.

Status: **[x] modelled** · **[~] approximation** · **[ ] open**.

## Massing

- [x] Site: the NYC footprint, 65.7 × 115.6 m between Mercer St, Bleecker St and W Houston St (D).
- [x] Podium of six storeys over the whole site, 27 m (W; height K).
- [x] Faculty tower at the Houston St end, 91 m, 23 floors (W), with two shallow setbacks near the top and a mechanical box (C pc_0, pc_009).
- [x] Student residence towers at the Bleecker St end, 68.6 m, 16 floors (W); a second, lower block beside it (C pc_mercer).
- [~] Tower plans (which part of the site each covers) are read from the photos and aerial imagery (K).

## Facades

- [x] Podium: reflective glass with slim dark vertical fins and dark floor bands (C pc_mercer, pc_0).
- [x] Ground floor set back under the cantilevered podium with a dark soffit and corner columns; the name JOHN A. PAULSON CENTER / NEW YORK UNIVERSITY in tall letters down the corner mullion at Mercer and Bleecker (C pc_006). No logo.
- [x] Faculty tower: green-tinted glass with fine light fins and mullion grid (C pc_009, pc_0).
- [x] Student towers: a pixelated stack of glass boxes on a 4.2 m grid; some boxes left out as dark notches, some slid 1.5–3 m out over the edge with copper-orange undersides (C pc_002, pc_004, pc_008, pc_mercer). The pattern is generated, not copied box by box.
- [ ] The podium's stepped glass tiers on the Houston St side (C pc_0) and the green roofs.
- [ ] Citi Bike dock on Mercer St (inventory) belongs to the street-furniture pass.
