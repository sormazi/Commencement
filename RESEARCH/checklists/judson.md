# Judson Memorial Church and campanile: detail checklist

Model: `dist/campus/landmarks/judson.js` (BIN 1008717, 55 Washington Square South at Thompson St; Judson Hall BIN 1082205 next door). McKim, Mead & White; church 1888–93, campanile 1895–96 (LPC LP-0196; Wikipedia).
Local frame: origin at the Washington Sq S / Thompson St corner, +x west along the square, +z north.

Status: **[x] modelled** · **[~] approximation / estimate** · **[ ] open**. Sources: LPC = LP-0196 description; C = Commons (judson_east, judson_west, judson_campanile by Beyond My Ken 2011; judson_square by Coelacanth2014 2016); D = NYC data.

## Proportions

- [x] North front 19.7 m wide (5 bays), east side on Thompson St 30.8 m (7 bays, LPC), from the footprint.
- [x] Eave 12.8 m, pediment apex 20.2 m (3D model roof 20.6 m incl. the cross base), measured on C judson_east.
- [~] Campanile 7.5 m square, about 40 m to the cross: **estimate** scaled on Judson Hall's surveyed 21 m cornice in C judson_west; the 3D model does not carry the tower.

## Church (LPC wording in quotes)

- [x] "Bands of terra-cotta ornament alternating with two courses of recessed yellow Roman brick" around the ground storey of the north and east fronts, with rectangular ground-floor windows.
- [x] North front: "three round-headed stained glass windows placed between brick pilasters and symmetrically balanced by round-headed brick panels"; "below the sills are marble panels enframed in terra-cotta mouldings".
- [x] East front: "slender brick pilasters placed between seven round-headed stained glass windows".
- [x] Pilaster capitals and bases, terra-cotta archivolts, entablature with frieze, dentil and modillion cornice.
- [x] "Low-pitched pediment" with raking cornices, central rose window with spokes and two roundels (C judson_east).
- [x] Copper cross on the apex; low-pitched gable roof.

## Porch between church and tower

- [x] "Two story entrance doorway set between the Tower and the Church": arched portico on two columns, coffered arch (as a deep reveal), "five half round steps", dark panelled doors.
- [x] "Low pitched tile roof" over the porch.

## Campanile

- [x] Banded base, plain shaft with small windows, three arcaded stages with dentilled stage cornices (centre arch open, flanking arches blind), open belfry loggia of three arches per face with columns, deep crowning cornice with modillions, low pyramidal tile roof, cross (C judson_west, judson_campanile).

## Neighbour
