## Massing (D)

- [x] Nine-storey slab at 29.8 m with the two upper blocks the residence calls the East and West towers (32.6 m and 34.6 m in the 3D model) and the low dining wing in the courtyard (8.9 m).
- [x] From University Pl and Waverly Pl the towers read as one continuous slab (SV): they rise only about a storey above the main roof.

## Facade (SV)

- [x] Orange-red brick.
- [x] Regular grid of single windows on a 3.05 m pitch, grey frames, transom, louvre panel at the bottom of each window.
- [x] Ground storey: light concrete piers, storefront glazing, concrete fascia band (the dining hall and market frontage on University Pl).
- [x] Three segmental concrete canopies over the residence entrance a few bays in from the E 8th St corner, with dark security grilles behind.
- [x] NYU banner on a short pole at the second floor near the corner (plain violet, no emblem).
- [~] Card readers and doors behind the glass: the doors read as dark grilles and glass from the street; no readers are distinguishable at street distance in SV, so none are modelled.
- [~] Dining hall entrance: modelled as part of the University Pl storefront run; SV does not show a separately marked dining entrance on the street.
