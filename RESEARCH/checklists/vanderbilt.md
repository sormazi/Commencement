# Vanderbilt Hall: detail checklist

Model: `dist/campus/landmarks/vanderbilt.js` (BIN 1008716, 40 Washington Square South, NYU School of Law). Eggers & Higgins, 1948–51. Neo-Georgian.
Volume from the NYC 3D Building Model (2014). Sources: C = Commons photos (`RESEARCH/reference/commons/`, vh_*); SV = Google Street View, looked at, not saved; D = NYC data; K = estimate.
New reusable masonry kit in `facade.js`: `sashWindows`, `sashTexture`, `modillionCornice`, `pediment`, `archHole` (for the second-tier pass).

Status: **[x] modelled** · **[~] approximation** · **[ ] open**.

## Massing

- [x] U-shaped block round a garden court open to Washington Sq S; wings and ranges at 22.6 m, the central cross range at 26.3 m, lower rear wings at 7.5–15.5 m (D).
- [x] Five storeys (D PLUTO): 5.6 m ground storey, 4.0 m floors (K, fitted to the 22.6 m eave).

## Facades

- [x] Red brick with limestone trim (C vh_1, vh_2, vh_gv019). Granite base course.
- [x] Six-over-six sash windows with white frames and limestone sills; smaller attic windows on the 26.3 m range. Bay pitch 3.3 m (K).
- [x] Limestone bands at the second-floor sill (level with the arcade coping) and under the top floor (C vh_1, vh_gv019).
- [x] Modillion cornice at the eave.
- [x] Wing ends on the square: a single central column of recessed blind panels, no windows (C vh_1), and a pediment with an oculus above. At ground level, the west wing has an open round-arched doorway and the east wing a blind arch with an oval stone tablet (C vh_1).
- [x] Pedimented centres on MacDougal St and Sullivan St (SV Apr 2026: 131 MacDougal, heading 95; 245 Sullivan, heading 280), with the fanlit double door and lantern under the MacDougal one (C vh_mmda).
- [~] Pediment width (14 m) and the oculus (a lunette in the photos) are estimates.

## Arcade and court

- [x] One-storey arcade wall closing the court along the square: five round arches, the middle one the entrance with steps; the others on a granite base with iron railings (C vh_1, vh_entr, vh_arches).
- [x] Limestone imposts and keystones, limestone coping (C vh_entr).
- [x] "40" street number, the two bronze plaques (ARTHUR T. VANDERBILT HALL; NEW YORK UNIVERSITY SCHOOL OF LAW) and the two lanterns beside the entrance (C vh_entr).
- [x] Doorcase across the court: limestone pilasters, entablature and pediment over a fanlit door (C vh_entr).
- [x] A plain violet banner on the Sullivan St corner. The NYU torch and the NYU Law wordmark are not drawn.
