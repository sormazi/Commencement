# Washington Square · NYU

A free-roam reconstruction of NYU's Washington Square campus a hundred years from now. It is the default location. Times Square, SoHo and Shibuya are unchanged repeating corridors.

**Status: Phase 0 (world model) done; Phase 1 inventory awaiting review.** Buildings are clay massing at their surveyed roof heights. No facades, decay, crowds, billboards or soundtrack yet.

## World model

- `dist/campus/projection.js`: local equirectangular projection, metres, origin at the centre of the Washington Square Arch. Map points are `[east, north]`; physics uses x = east, z = north; Three.js uses z = -north.
- `dist/campus/data/campus-data.js`: generated by `tools/build-campus-data.mjs` from the raw extracts listed in `RESEARCH/data/SOURCES.md`. 4,250 building footprints, 112 streets / 577 centerline segments, roadbed, sidewalk, median and plaza polygons, ~5,000 trees, lamps, benches, fences, monuments.
- `dist/campus/streets.js`: street graph from NYC Centerline with real curb-to-curb widths, lane counts and travel direction; nearest-street lookup for the HUD and minimap; route queries for tests.
- `dist/campus/collision.js`: static collision in a uniform grid. Buildings, fences and walls are segments; trees, statues and the flagpole are circles; the Arch is two piers with the real 30 ft opening; the fountain rim is solid. The car is three circles along its axis and contacts go through the existing `resolveContact`. A surface query gives the solver curb height (sidewalks and park paving sit 15 cm above the roadbed) and lower grip on lawns.
- `dist/campus/campus-world.js`: Three.js world streamed in 120 m tiles. Each tile merges its walls, roofs, slabs and lawns into a few meshes, and instances its trees, lamps, benches, fence panels and crosswalk bars. Tiles beyond 560 m and props beyond 330 m are hidden.
- `dist/campus/minimap.js`: heading-up street map with street names, park, NYU buildings in violet and a north arrow. The HUD shows the street (or "Washington Square Park") under the map.
- Spawn: on Fifth Avenue north of Washington Square North, facing south through the Arch. Reset (R) puts the car back on the nearest street, keeping its heading.

The car can drive on every road, on sidewalks and through the park's paved paths. Buildings, fences, the Arch piers, the fountain rim, trees and monuments are solid. There is no traffic in this location.

## What is accurate

- Street layout, carriageway outlines, curbs, sidewalks and medians come straight from NYC planimetric data (accurate to well under a metre).
- Building footprints and roof heights come from NYC Building Footprints (photogrammetric). Heights are main-roof heights; parapets, bulkheads and setbacks are not modelled yet.
- Park lawns, playgrounds, dog runs, fences, benches, lamps and monuments are placed from OpenStreetMap; trees from NYC Parks Forestry data plus OSM.

## What is approximate or missing

- The world is flat. Real ground elevation varies by a few metres across the area; it is ignored.
- Every building is a vertical extrusion with a generic window texture. Setbacks, roof forms, materials and details arrive in Phase 2 (NYC 3D Building Model, then facades).
- The Arch is massing only (piers and attic). The fountain is a plain ring.
- Benches have no orientation yet; lamp posts and fences use one generic style.
- Crosswalks come from OSM and are incomplete; there are no lane markings yet.
- Retaining walls around the fountain plaza are drawn but not solid, pending confirmation of the plaza's level.
- Performance has only been checked in software WebGL in a headless browser (not representative). It has not been profiled on a real mid-range laptop yet.

## Developer helpers

In the browser console: `NightView.teleport(x, z, yaw)` moves the car to map metres (x east, z north, yaw 0 = north). `NightView.viewFrom({x, y, n, lx, ly, ln, fov, fog, view})` fixes the camera for review screenshots; `NightView.viewFrom(null)` returns to the chase camera.
